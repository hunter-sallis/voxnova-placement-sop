The rule that changes the order

The artist signs the SoundExchange Letter of Direction BEFORE the producer signs the producer agreement.

Once the producer signs, the artist side has no reason to hurry on the LOD. So the producer's signature waits.

Three sentences to remember

  • A request is not proof. "Sent," "signed," and "OK to pay" are claims. The document is the proof.
  • Never choose between two versions. If two documents say different things, 🚨 escalate.
  • Never touch money or signatures. You don't sign, send bank details, or agree to terms.

06LOD / SoundExchange ChecklistOpen

What it is: the artist signs a Letter of Direction (LOD) telling SoundExchange to pay part of the artist's digital-radio royalties to the producer's company. Only the artist can sign it.

Why it comes first: the producer's signature waits until the artist's LOD is in. That's the only leverage we have to get it.

Part A — Get the artist's LOD (before the producer signs)

☐ LOD requested from the artist's attorney/label (Email 03) — send it with the contracting info, not after release
☐ Producer company + % on the LOD confirmed from the agreement
☐ Song title / recording information confirmed
☐ Producer + attorney told to hold signature
☐ LOD sent to the artist for e-signature (save the notice)
☐ Artist-signed LOD PDF saved
☐ Certificate of completion saved

Part B — Check the certificate (all must pass)

☐ It's the e-signature platform's own completion page (not a screenshot of an email)
☐ Document ID or document name matches the LOD
☐ Signer = the artist named on the LOD
☐ Status says Completed, with a date/time
☐ Signature is a real e-signature or ink, not a pasted image
☐ Payee company on the LOD = the agreement
☐ % on the LOD = the agreement
☐ Names and titles spelled correctly
All pass → the producer may sign (card 04, Part C). Any fail → Email 03, "correction" version. Wrong payee or % → 🚨 Escalate.

Part C — After the agreement is fully executed

☐ Who submits the LOD to SoundExchange, confirmed in writing
☐ LOD submitted — proof saved (email, case number, or dated screenshot)
☐ Active at SoundExchange — SoundExchange email or dated portal screenshot
☐ Everything uploaded to 04_SoundExchange_LOD/
Check "active" every 10 business days. Not active after 3 checks → 🚨 Escalate.

What doesn't count

They say / you have It is
"I sent the LOD" Sent — not signed
"He signed it" Nothing until the PDF is saved
Signed LOD, no certificate Not verified
Signed LOD + certificate Verified — but not proof SoundExchange has it

If it's missing

ACTION REQUIRED: Request LOD documentation → Email 03.

11Employee Do / Don'tOpen

✅ DO

  • Save the proof before you change any stage
  • Copy terms word for word into the record, with the date and sender
  • Write UNKNOWN when no document says it
  • Keep every version. Make a new file; never overwrite
  • Open the attachment. Take facts from the document, not from the email describing it
  • Keep the producer's signature on hold until the artist's LOD + certificate are in
  • Ask every co-producer to confirm the split in writing
  • Start vendor paperwork early, at TERMS
  • Follow the clock: +3 days → follow-up · +3 → final follow-up · +2 → escalate
  • Reply on the same thread so the history stays together
  • Update "Next action" and "Due" every time you touch a placement
  • Review every open placement on Monday
  • Escalate the same day when a card says 🚨

❌ DON'T

  • Don't negotiate or suggest terms, even small ones
  • Don't pick between two conflicting numbers
  • Don't sign anything for anyone
  • Don't send or ask for bank details, tax IDs, or ID documents by email (they go on the label's own forms)
  • Don't change a payment route or accept a new one
  • Don't release signature pages before the LOD gate is cleared
  • Don't mark anything done because someone said so ("sent," "signed," "OK to pay")
  • Don't tell a label whether it can release a song
  • Don't give legal, tax, or accounting opinions. "I'll check with my manager" is always a good answer
  • Don't send a third follow-up. Escalate instead
  • Don't share placement records or contacts outside Vox Nova, the producer, and the producer's attorney
  • Don't "fix" a document yourself (spelling, payee, amounts). Ask the sender to correct it

When in doubt

STOP → DOCUMENT → ESCALATE

Overview · SOP.md

SOP — Producer Placement → LOD → Contract → Payment → Release

Licensor: Keyman Publishing, LLC · Licensee: Vox Nova, LLC Applies to: every song where a Vox Nova producer client is credited as a producer Version: 1.0 — 2026-09-30

1. Purpose

Take a placement from the first email to a closed file, and be able to prove every step with a document. The operator's job is not to believe that something happened. It is to collect the proof that moves the placement to its next status.

2. Operational controls, not legal advice

This SOP is an operations checklist. It does not interpret contracts, decide what terms are fair, or give legal, tax or accounting advice. Whenever this SOP says "compare" or "reconcile," the operator checks that the words and numbers are the same. Deciding whether a difference is acceptable belongs to the producer and their attorney, through the Escalation Owner.

The operator never: - signs anything, for anyone - agrees to, proposes or changes any deal term - releases a producer's signature pages - gives payment or bank details, or changes a payment route - tells a label the song can or can't be released - receives or forwards producer advances

Status Definitions & Transition Rules

Every placement has exactly one Placement Status at a time, plus two parallel Track Statuses (Payment, SoundExchange) that open after the agreement is fully executed.

A status moves forward only when the evidence in the "Required to enter" column is saved in the placement folder (see ../SOP.md §6 Document Control). An email that describes an event is not evidence of the event.

1. Placement Status (main line)

# Status Meaning Required to ENTER this status (evidence)
P0 NEW Something about a possible placement came in. Placement record created with source email/file saved to 99_Correspondence/.
P0-U UNPAPERED Only an audio file, title match, or verbal mention exists — no commercial terms in writing. Record created; "What should be verified next" filled in. Stays here until written terms arrive.
P1 TERMS REVIEW Written deal terms received; being extracted and checked. Term Snapshot #1 saved (01_Deal_Terms/) with the exact quoted sentence for each term.
P2 TERMS CONFIRMED Every producer on the song has confirmed the same terms in writing, and samples/collaborators are answered. (a) Written "confirmed" reply from each producer or their rep, referencing the snapshot version; (b) written sample answer from the producer side (none / list); (c) written collaborator answer; (d) no open Contradiction Flag.
P3 CONTRACTING Contracting info delivered; agreement being drafted / negotiated. Contracting-info email sent (saved) and acknowledged, or first draft agreement received.
P4 EXECUTION VERSION RECEIVED The final, clean agreement for signature is in hand and reconciled to confirmed terms. Execution-version PDF saved in 03_Agreement/ + Terms Reconciliation completed with every field = MATCH (or each difference approved in writing by the Escalation Owner).
P5 LOD GATE — HOLD Waiting for the artist-signed SoundExchange LOD before the producer signs. Producer must NOT sign in this status. Execution version reconciled (P4). Applies whenever LOD Required = YES.
P6 LOD RECEIVED Artist-signed LOD and its certificate of completion are in hand and verified. (a) LOD PDF with artist signature; (b) e-signature Certificate of Completion for that envelope; (c) LOD Verification (workflow soundexchange-lod.md §4) all PASS.
P7 PRODUCER SIGNED Producer has signed the agreement. Producer-signed PDF and the e-signature certificate (or scanned wet-ink pages). A drawn/pasted image signature does not count.
P8 FULLY EXECUTED All parties have signed. Fully executed PDF showing every signature line signed (including inducement letters and exhibits that require signatures). "OK to pay", "we'll circle back with the executed copy", or a partially executed copy do not count.
P9 RELEASE READY Everything required before release is done. Pre-release gate (../checklists/release-readiness-checklist.md) = all required items PASS.
P10 COMPLETE Placement fully closed. Payment Track = PAID-CONFIRMED and SoundExchange Track = ACTIVE-CONFIRMED (or N/A with reason) and evidence package audit passed and registration hand-off sent.

Side statuses (can be set from any P-status)

Status When to set How to leave it
BLOCKED Any required item can't move without someone else acting and a follow-up rule has run out, or an escalation trigger fires. Record the blocker in one sentence. Blocker resolved with evidence → return to the prior P-status.
ESCALATED An escalation trigger in ../workflows/escalation.md fires. Escalation Owner writes a decision in the record → return to the prior P-status or move to CLOSED.
RELEASED WITH OUTSTANDING ITEMS The song came out before P9. (This happened in all 3 of the papered historical cases.) Keep the P-status too, e.g. P7 / RELEASED WITH OUTSTANDING ITEMS. Reach P10.
CLOSED — NOT PLACED Written confirmation that the song won't use the producer's work, or the Escalation Owner closes it. —

Rules that never bend

  1. No producer signature before P6 when LOD Required = YES. If anyone asks the producer to sign first, escalate (trigger E-10).
  2. No skipping. P5 → P7 is not allowed. P3 → P7 is not allowed.
  3. Terms change = back to P1. If any term changes after P2, create a new Term Snapshot, set a Contradiction Flag, and go back to TERMS REVIEW.
  4. Status only moves on evidence. If the evidence isn't in the folder, the status doesn't move, whatever the email says.

Workflow 3 — SoundExchange Letter of Direction (LOD)

What this is (operations view)

A SoundExchange LOD is signed by the featured artist. It directs SoundExchange to pay a share of the artist's digital-performance royalties to the producer's named payee. Only the artist can sign it, so the producer depends on the artist side to get it done. That's why Vox Nova requires it before the producer signs the producer agreement.

Whether an LOD is due, and what percentage, is set by the producer agreement. The operator doesn't decide either one.

Timing rule

P3 CONTRACTING ── request the LOD (Step 1)
P4 EXECUTION VERSION ── LOD wording in the agreement reconciled
P5 LOD GATE — HOLD ── producer does NOT sign
P6 LOD RECEIVED ── artist-signed LOD + certificate verified
P7 PRODUCER SIGNS

Step 1 — Request (at P3) → LOD REQUESTED

Who sends Operator
To Artist attorney (or label business affairs if the artist attorney isn't known), cc producer attorney
Template templates/lod-request.md Version A
Asks for (1) the LOD form SoundExchange will accept, filled in with the producer's payee entity and % from the agreement; (2) the artist's signature through a verified e-signature platform; (3) the Certificate of Completion for that envelope; (4) confirmation of who will submit it to SoundExchange
Attachments Payee details sheet (entity legal name, contact email, and the SoundExchange account holder name, if known). No bank details.
Complete when The other side confirms in writing who is preparing the LOD
No response Standard clock (3 / 3 / 2 BD) → E-13

Step 2 — Sent for signature → LOD SENT FOR SIGNATURE

Evidence: e-sign envelope notification, or the other side's email saying it was sent, with the date. Save it. This is not a signature.

Step 3 — Signed → LOD SIGNED BY ARTIST

Evidence: the LOD PDF with the artist's signature, saved as YYYY-MM-DD_LOD_<Artist>_SIGNED.pdf. If only "he signed it" arrives, reply with lod-request.md Version B (request the PDF + certificate).

Step 4 — Certificate verification → CERTIFICATE VERIFIED (= P6)

Save the Certificate of Completion as YYYY-MM-DD_LOD-Certificate_<Artist>.pdf, then fill in this sheet and save it as LOD-Verification.md:

# Check PASS / FAIL
1 The certificate is the platform's own audit/completion page (not a screenshot of an email)
2 The envelope/document ID on the certificate matches the LOD PDF (or the certificate lists the LOD document name)
3 The signer name on the certificate = the featured artist named on the LOD
4 The status shows Completed, with a completion timestamp
5 The signature on the LOD is a platform e-signature or wet ink — not a pasted image
6 Payee entity on the LOD = the SoundExchange payee in the executed/execution agreement
7 The % on the LOD = the % in the agreement
8 Song title(s) and artist name are spelled correctly; ISRC present if the form asks for it
9 Retroactive/effective-date wording matches the agreement (if the agreement says anything about it)
10 Names are spelled exactly as in the contracting info (historical C3: a payment-direction letter had to be re-signed over a misspelled name)

Any FAIL → send lod-request.md Version C (correction request) and stay in P5. Checks 6, 7 or 9 FAIL → also escalate E-11.

All PASS → P6 LOD RECEIVED. Tell the producer's attorney that the signature hold is lifted.

Step 5 — Submission to SoundExchange → SUBMITTED TO SOUNDEXCHANGE

After P8, confirm in writing who submits the LOD to SoundExchange (the artist side, the artist's attorney, or the producer side). Evidence: the submission email to SoundExchange, a SoundExchange case/ticket email, or a dated portal screenshot. No proof within 10 BD of P6 → FU1 to whoever agreed to submit.

Step 6 — Active confirmation → ACTIVE-CONFIRMED

Evidence: a SoundExchange email or dated portal screenshot showing the LOD active for the payee entity. Check every 10 BD until confirmed. After 3 checks with no confirmation → escalate E-11.

A signed LOD + certificate is not proof that SoundExchange has it on file. In a separate engagement, signed LODs existed that SoundExchange did not have.

Situation Action

LOD can't be obtained after FU2 E-11
Certificate missing but the signed PDF exists Stay in P5; Version B; E-11 at the end of the clock

Workflow 4 — Payment (Payment Track)

Principles

  • Vox Nova tracks payment. It doesn't receive, hold or redirect a producer's advance unless a signed agreement says so.
  • One obligation = one row: producer advance, legal-fee invoice, any other invoice.
  • The amount due comes from the executed agreement, not from an email.
  • Payment is confirmed only by a remittance or bank record showing amount + payer + date, matched to the invoice.

Step 1 — Start vendor setup (at P2) → VENDOR SETUP IN PROGRESS

Historical cases show vendor paperwork holding up payment after the contract was done (C2 outdated tax form; C3 form rejected for its signature type and form version). Start it early.

The producer (or their entity signer) completes and signs using a verified e-signature platform or wet ink. Notarization / ID only if the form asks. Completed forms saved Producer or attorney submits
Check that the tax form (W-9 or equivalent) is current and in the payee entity's exact name Check noted in the record —
Label confirms the vendor record is created/approved Confirmation saved VENDOR SETUP CONFIRMED

Rejection → save the rejection, note the reason in the record, fix only what the label named, and resubmit on the form version the label sent with the rejection.

Step 2 — Confirm the payment route → ROUTE CONFIRMED

Check PASS when
Payee entity on the invoice = payee in the agreement's payment-direction letter (if any) = vendor record All three the same

Workflow 5 — Pre-Release Gate (→ P9 RELEASE READY)

What the gate does and doesn't do

The gate decides whether Vox Nova marks the placement RELEASE READY in its own records. It does not stop a label from releasing a song, and the operator never tells a label whether it can release. In every papered historical case (C1, C2, C3) the song came out before the paperwork was finished, so the gate's practical job is to escalate early and to record honestly what was outstanding when the song came out.

When to run it

  • Every time the release date is learned or changes
  • Weekly for any placement with a release date
  • Daily once the release is ≤10 BD away
  • On release day

The gate

Use ../checklists/release-readiness-checklist.md. Each line is PASS, FAIL or N/A (N/A only with the reason and the Escalation Owner's initials).

# Item PASS evidence
1 Terms confirmed P2 confirmations saved; Contradiction Flag CLEARED
2 Producer identities confirmed Every producer named, with a contracting entity; no unknown producer
3 Publishing confirmed Share stated as each or total, in the executed agreement
4 Master points confirmed Points/royalty + allocation in the executed agreement
5 Samples cleared / confirmed none Producer's written answer; any sample has a clearance record from the label
6 Other collaborators identified Written collaborator answer; no new names since
7 Artist-signed LOD + certificate verified before the producer signed P6 date ≤ P7 date
8 Contract fully executed P8 PDF, every signature line
9 Payment status confirmed PAID-CONFIRMED, or vendor setup confirmed + route confirmed + invoice acknowledged, with written Escalation Owner acceptance that payment follows release
10 LOD submitted to SoundExchange Submission proof saved
11 Documents stored Folder audit (SOP §6.4) passes for everything done so far
12 No unresolved producer-rights issue No open escalation; no open Contradiction Flag

Result

  • All PASS (or approved N/A) → set P9 RELEASE READY.
  • Any FAIL → the record shows:
BLOCKED — DO NOT MARK RELEASE READY
Failing items: #__, #__
Release date: YYYY-MM-DD (__ BD away)
Owner of each item: …

and: - release >10 BD away → run the normal follow-up clocks - release ≤10 BD away → escalate E-12 the same day, and send templates/pre-release-clearance.md to the label/artist attorney only if the Escalation Owner approves - release has happened → set RELEASED WITH OUTSTANDING ITEMS, list the open items, and keep working the clocks toward P10

Note: item 10 (submission) can PASS without SoundExchange confirming the LOD is active. Active confirmation is required for P10 COMPLETE, not P9.

Workflow 6 — Escalation

How to escalate

  1. Set the side status ESCALATED and log it in 00_Record/escalations.md:
E-ID: E-__   Raised: YYYY-MM-DD   Placement: KM-____ — Artist — Song
Trigger: (one sentence)
Evidence: (file names)
Options I can see: (optional — facts only, no recommendation on terms)
Deadline pressure: release date / promised date
Decision needed by: YYYY-MM-DD
  1. Send it to the Escalation Owner (README → Settings) the same business day, with the evidence files attached or linked.
  2. Don't take any outside action on the escalated point until the decision is written in the record. Other steps on the placement keep running.
  3. No decision in 2 BD → resend, marked "second request." No decision in 2 BD more → raise it at the next weekly review, first item.

Triggers

ID Trigger Operator does NOT…
E-01 Deal terms conflict across emails, snapshots or parties (incl. "each" vs "total" publishing) pick the version that looks right
E-02 A producer's identity or contracting entity can't be verified guess the entity
E-03 Publishing percentages don't add up across all writers/producers adjust anyone's share
E-04 Master points / royalty don't add up adjust the allocation
E-05 A new collaborator, writer or producer appears add them to the split
E-06 A sample or interpolation is discovered describe its clearance status to the label
E-07 Execution version differs from confirmed terms, or an exhibit is missing / "to be provided separately" send the producer's signature
E-08 Payment amount ≠ agreed amount, payer unclear, or a duplicate payment tell anyone to return or keep money
E-09 More than one payment route, a payment-direction letter to a third party, or a bank-detail change submit any vendor/bank form
E-10 Anyone asks the producer to sign before the artist-signed LOD + certificate arrive release the hold
E-11 LOD can't be obtained, certificate missing after FU2, LOD payee/% ≠ agreement, or not active after 3 checks edit or resend the LOD
E-12 Release ≤10 BD away with any pre-release gate item FAIL, or the song released while items were open tell the label to stop or delay
E-13 No response after Follow-Up #2 on any request send a Follow-Up #3 on their own
E-14 Label, artist team or co-producer disputes the terms argue the terms
E-15 Anyone asks Vox Nova to sign, accept terms, give bank details or receive funds do it
E-16 A document has a misspelled name or wrong entity "fix" the document themselves

Priority

Priority Triggers Owner response expected
Urgent (same day) E-10, E-12, E-09, E-15 Same BD
High E-01, E-05, E-06, E-07, E-08, E-11, E-14 ≤2 BD
Normal E-02, E-03, E-04, E-13, E-16 ≤2 BD

Legal line

When the decision needs a legal, tax or accounting judgment, the Escalation Owner routes it to the producer's attorney or accountant. The operator writes down the decision and who made it, never the reasoning behind a legal position.

Historical Basis — Why the SOP Is Built This Way

This SOP was built from four email audits of past producer placements (Sep 2026). People's names and deal amounts are left out on purpose; the full audits are kept separately by the Escalation Owner.

Case Placement What the record showed
C1 Case 1 (major label, 2025–26) Terms by email in 6 days. Execution version arrived 4 months later. Producer signed Jan 20; fully executed copy 46 days after that. Song was already out before execution. Invoice sent 54 days after execution; 11 weeks after the invoice, repeated follow-ups still had no payment and no reply. No SoundExchange LOD ever started. No W-9 request found. Publishing % never stated in email.
C2 Case 2 (major label, 2024) Terms changed across 3 versions; the publishing wording contradicted itself ("25% each" vs "25% split evenly") and was never resolved in writing. The label asked for sample details; the producer side never answered in writing. Agreement came 5 months after terms; producer signed the next day; no fully executed copy found. Co-producer's invoice got a rush; the producer's invoice got no reply. A label tax-portal notice said the tax form was outdated. No LOD ever raised.
C3 Case 3 (major label, 2026) Artist's attorney wouldn't draft for 63 days until the co-producer's team confirmed the equal split; the producer's attorney followed up about 11 times. Producer signed before the artist's LOD arrived, then asked for the LOD certificate of completion 3 times (Aug 3, Aug 31, Sep 10). None received. Producer's signed pages sat 28 days without being received. Label vendor form rejected (drawn signature, outdated form version). Two payment routes ran at once (contract payee vs a separate payment-direction letter), and one of those letters needed re-signing because the name was misspelled. "OK to pay" Sep 10 → PO Sep 21 → "should land Wednesday" Sep 28 → no receipt found. Song released and later taken down while unpapered.
C4 Case 4 (independent) Only an audio file. No terms, contract, invoice or LOD in the mailbox. Possible terms sit inside publisher amendment and statement attachments that nobody has opened. A different song with the same title exists.

Lessons → controls

Lesson (case) Control in this SOP
Producer signed first and lost all leverage to get the artist's LOD (C3). LOD GATE (P5): the artist-signed LOD + certificate must be received before the producer signs.
LOD never raised at all (C1, C2). LOD Required defaults to YES; LOD is requested when contracting starts, not after release.
Signed LODs are not the same as LODs on file at SoundExchange (seen in a separate engagement). SoundExchange Track ends at ACTIVE-CONFIRMED, not at "signed."
Co-producer split unconfirmed → 63-day drafting stall (C3). P2 requires written confirmation from every producer before contracting info goes out.
Terms drifted across versions; publishing contradiction never fixed (C2). Numbered Term Snapshots, never overwritten; Contradiction Flag blocks P2.
Sample question unanswered by producer side (C2). P2 requires the producer's own written sample answer.
"Fully executed" assumed from an email; PDF never opened (C1). Fully executed copy never received (C2, C3). P8 requires the fully executed PDF with every signature line checked.
Signed pages lost in transit for 28 days (C3). After producer signature: confirm receipt within 2 business days.
Vendor form rejected for signature type and outdated form (C3); tax form outdated (C2). Vendor setup starts at P2; verified e-signature only; always use the form version the label sends this time.
Two payment routes at once; misspelled name on a payment-direction letter (C3). ROUTE CONFIRMED status; any second route = escalation E-09; name check on every signed document.
"OK to pay", PO, "should land" treated as progress (C3). PAYMENT PROMISED ≠ PAID-CONFIRMED.
Invoice silence for 11 weeks (C1); co-producer paid first (C2). Fixed payment follow-up clock; escalate at FU2.
Songs released before paperwork in every papered case (C1, C2, C3). Release-date countdown triggers escalation at 10 business days; RELEASED WITH OUTSTANDING ITEMS status.
Audio-only "placement" (C4). UNPAPERED status; never assume a deal exists.
Facts inside unopened attachments (C1, C2, C4). Terms are taken from the executed PDF, not from email summaries.

Where the follow-up timing comes from

The historical record shows long silences (weeks to months), not a working cadence, so the timers in this SOP are Vox Nova policy, set tighter than history on purpose:

  • Standard request: FU1 at 3 business days, FU2 3 business days later, escalate 2 business days after FU2 (8 business days total).
  • Payment: first check 10 business days after the invoice is acknowledged (or on the date the label promised, if sooner).
  • Release countdown: escalate at ≤10 business days to release with any gate item open.

The Escalation Owner can change these in ../README.md → Settings.
